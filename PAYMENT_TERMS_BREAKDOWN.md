## FAQ - Payment Terms

**Q: Can we pay the full amount upfront?**  
A: Yes, and we can provide a 2% discount on total project cost if paid upfront.

**Q: What if UAT takes longer?**  
A: Payment milestones are tied to deliverable completion, not calendar dates. Each milestone is released upon specific criteria being met.

**Q: Are there any hidden costs?**  
A: No, the total cost is ₹20,00,000 + 18% GST. No additional charges except for any scope changes requested by client.

**Q: What happens if project is delayed?**  
A: Payment milestones remain tied to deliverable completion. UAT and Go Live payments are released when those phases are successfully completed.

**Q: Is there a retention amount?**  
A: No retention is built into this payment structure. However, post-launch support for 30 days is included.

**Q: What about GST?**  
A: 18% GST is applicable as per current Indian tax regulations. Total with GST: ₹23,60,000
