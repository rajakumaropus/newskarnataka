## 🎯 THE OPPORTUNITY

### What We're Building
An **AI-enabled news platform for Karnataka** combining:
- **Customer App (Phase 1):** Location-first mobile/web news experience
- **Editorial Console (Phase 2):** Groq-powered content validation system

### Why This Matters
- **First-mover advantage** in location-based news for Karnataka
- **Groq AI** enables real-time validation at sustainable cost ($0.35 per 1000 stories)
- **Real estate advertising** market ripe for digital penetration
- **User demand** for interactive, engaging news experiences

### Market Position
- Not competing as commodity news app (like Google News)
- Competing as **engagement platform** with real estate focus
- Targeting **professional users** (property seekers) as secondary audience
- Enabling **editorial efficiency** through Groq AI

**Risk:** Cannot deliver by 8 weeks  
**Mitigation:** Experienced team, daily standups, ruthless scope control  
**Probability:** Low (aggressive but achievable)

### Risk 3: Groq API Issues (Severity: MEDIUM)
**Risk:** API downtime or accuracy problems  
**Mitigation:** Caching, fallback rules, Claude backup, SLA enforcement  
**Probability:** Low

### Risk 4: Team Attrition (Severity: HIGH)
**Risk:** Key people leave during development  
**Mitigation:** Competitive salaries, equity incentives, milestone bonuses  
**Probability:** Medium

### Risk 5: Market Competition (Severity: MEDIUM)
**Risk:** Competitors enter market during development  
**Mitigation:** First-mover advantage, location moat, continuous innovation  
**Probability:** Medium (but 8-week advantage is significant)
