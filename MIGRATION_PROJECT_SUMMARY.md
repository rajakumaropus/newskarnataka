## 🚀 IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

### Strategy: Safe & Gradual (10 weeks)

**Why Safe & Gradual?**
- ✅ 55K articles = data complexity
- ✅ Production traffic = zero-downtime critical
- ✅ SEO = ranking preservation mandatory
- ✅ Editorial team = training needed
- ✅ Risk = carefully managed

**Why Not Faster?**
- Rushing: Data loss risk, quality issues
- 8 weeks: Possible but aggressive

**Why Not Slower?**

- Competitive window narrows
- Tech debt compounds
