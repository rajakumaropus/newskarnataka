## COMMON ISSUES & RESOLUTION

### Issue: Database Connection Fails
**Symptoms:** "psql: could not translate host name"  
**Cause:** Network connectivity or wrong credentials  
**Resolution:** Check VPN, firewall, credentials. Call Team C.

### Issue: Strapi Won't Start
**Symptoms:** Port 1337 already in use or connection errors  
**Cause:** Database not ready or wrong .env config  
**Resolution:** Check logs, verify .env, restart services

### Issue: Team Member Blocked
**Symptoms:** Can't proceed with work, waiting on other team  
**Cause:** Missing dependency or unclear requirements  
**Resolution:** Escalate to Tech Lead immediately

### Issue: Performance Degradation
**Symptoms:** Queries taking >100ms or APIs slow  
**Cause:** Missing indexes or inefficient queries  
**Resolution:** Analyze execution plans, add indexes
